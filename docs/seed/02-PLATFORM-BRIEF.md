# HIVE BRAIN — System Brief (Public)

**AdventureNLearn · AOS Control Plane**
**Class:** PUBLIC · **North star:** Replication · Fidelity (not engagement)
**Date:** 2026-08-10

## Purpose
AOS is the control plane for serious build work. Hive Brain is how we hold the whole picture: what the system is for, what ships publicly, and how claims stay honest.

This system is **not here for engagement**. It is here for **replication** and **fidelity** — so serious work can be repeated, audited, and continued without rewriting history or inflating claims for attention.

## In one paragraph
Imagine a workshop that builds public tools for civic learning and craft education, while protecting private research. Hive Brain is the workshop law, the filing system, and the inspection gate — so public products stay clean, private work stays private, and claims stay honest. Success is a product another team could rebuild from the record, not a spike in attention. A human still decides what is true enough to ship.

## Layers (rooms in one building)
1. **Control plane** — purpose and product discipline in one place.
2. **Knowledge (Hive)** — sources become linked knowledge that compounds.
3. **Reasoning** — structured scrutiny; models do not crown truth.
4. **Public products** — civic suite and Educational Tutor; plain language.
5. **Private research** — experiments stay fenced until deliberately promoted.
6. **Archive** — finished eras freeze for fidelity.

## Public products
- **Civic suite** — map-style tools for claims and jobsite-style surfaces; one production definition.
- **Educational Tutor** — craft learning; success is understanding that transfers, not time-on-page theater.

## Principles
- Replication over engagement
- Fidelity over narrative polish
- One integrity framework for every builder
- Separate control plane from products people use
- Keep public products free of private theater
- Ship nothing from tokens alone

## Integrity kernel
Supported / Unproven / Disputed · Evidence / Inference / Assumption · Human final call · Primary records beat commentary · Attention is not a truth signal.

## What it is not
Not an engagement farm · not auto-truth · not silent parallel production · not operator-runbook publishing · not finished by token spend or traction.

## How to judge success
- Could another team continue this from the record?
- Does the record stay honest?
- Are public tools clear and free of private theater?
- Were engagement metrics never used as the ship criterion?
