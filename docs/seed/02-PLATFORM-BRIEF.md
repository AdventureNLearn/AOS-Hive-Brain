# HIVE BRAIN — System Brief (Public) · v0.2.0

**AdventureNLearn · AOS workshop rules**
**Class:** PUBLIC · **Fixed rules:** Someone else can continue · Honest history (not popularity)
**Date:** 2026-08-10-r02

## Purpose
AOS is the workshop rules floor for serious build work. Hive Brain is how we hold the whole picture: what the system is for, what ships publicly, and how claims stay honest.

This system is **not here for popularity**. It is here so serious work can be **continued**, **checked**, and **saved honestly** — without rewriting history or inflating claims for attention.

## In one paragraph
Imagine a building with floors: public tools, private experiments, knowledge notes, and an archive. Hive Brain is the map of that building plus a living demo of honest decisions. A high-school senior and a senior engineer should both be able to walk the rooms. Success is a product another careful person could rebuild from the record — not a spike in attention. A human still decides what is true enough to share.

## Floors (rooms in one building)
1. **Workshop rules** — purpose and product discipline.
2. **Knowledge floor** — sources become linked notes that compound.
3. **Thinking** — structured scrutiny; tools do not crown truth alone.
4. **Public products** — civic tools and learning path; plain language.
5. **Private floor** — experiments stay fenced until deliberately promoted.
6. **Archive floor** — finished chapters freeze for honest history.

## Live demo (v0.2.0)
- **5 rooms · 20 shapes** (Orient, Integrity, Mission, Coordinate, Surface)
- **Shorter / Fuller** decision stories (moderate vs deep spine)
- Play is **main storyline only**; side drafts stay in the downloadable record
- **Node inspect**: hover (desktop) or tap (mobile) any node during play; pin one card; close with **X**
- Export full decision graph as JSON

## Public products
- **Civic tools** — map-style claims; plain language; labeled honesty.
- **Learning path** — craft learning; success is understanding that transfers, not watch-time.

## Principles
- Someone else can continue — over popularity
- Honest history — over a prettier story
- One honesty language for every builder
- Separate workshop rules from products people use
- Keep public tools free of private clutter
- Ship nothing from tokens alone

## Honesty kernel
Supported / Unproven / Disputed / Human decision · Evidence / careful guess / assumption · Original records beat commentary · Attention is not a truth signal.

## What it is not
Not a popularity farm · not auto-truth · not silent parallel production · not private-runbook publishing · not finished by token spend or traction.

## How to judge success
- Could another careful person continue this from the record?
- Does the written record stay honest when things get messy?
- Are public tools clear and free of private clutter?
- Were likes or watch-time never used as the reason to ship?
